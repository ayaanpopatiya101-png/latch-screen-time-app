## Description (4000 chars)

Latch turns "I should use my phone less" into a plan you can actually follow.

Meet Lumi — your AI screen-time coach. Lumi runs a short, conversational interview to learn how you use your phone, when you slip, and what pulls you back. From your answers, Latch builds a personalised plan: Focus windows that protect your best hours, a Shield that holds the line on apps you can't put down, and a daily credit system that rewards you for staying with the plan.

CONVERSATIONAL ONBOARDING
Lumi asks 6–8 questions in plain English — no sliders, no spreadsheets. Your Plan Power and Persona reveal the shape of your phone habit (Scroll Drifter, Late Night Owl, Doom Pinger, and more) and what to do about it.

SHIELD, THREE WAYS
• Gentle — a calm nudge when you cross your limit.
• Friction — a 20-second breath before the app opens. Most people stop right here.
• Deep Lock — the app is locked at the OS level until your window ends.

FOCUS WINDOWS THAT FIT YOUR DAY
Schedule "Morning Calm", "Deep Work", "Wind Down" — Latch shields the apps you choose during each block. Complete the window and you earn credits.

A CREDIT SYSTEM THAT ACTUALLY MOTIVATES
Stay under your limit? Credits. Complete a focus window? Credits. Want to unlock an app for 15 minutes? It costs credits — which means you decide whether it's worth it.

ON-DEVICE, PRIVACY-FIRST
Your raw screen-time data never leaves your phone. Latch uses Apple's Screen Time API and only sees aggregate signals. No ad tracking, no selling your data, ever. Delete your account in two taps from Settings.

WHO LATCH IS FOR
Adults who want to stop late-night scrolling, protect their focus at work, and feel calmer about their relationship with their phone — without parental-control gimmicks.
